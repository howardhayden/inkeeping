# Community conduct

Contributors and maintainers must communicate professionally, critique work rather than people, respect confidentiality and access needs, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, harassing, or sexualized conduct.

Do not post restricted records, personal data, credentials, vulnerability details, or institutional information without authority. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful material, restrict participation, and preserve evidence needed for a proportionate response.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an established nonpublic channel. Reports should be handled with minimum necessary access, documented decisions, protection against retaliation, and attention to conflicts of interest.
